Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how Shree Ram Tour & Travels (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit this website or book a vehicle or trip with us.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to share: your name, phone number, the trip and vehicle you are interested in, your travel date, and pickup and drop details when you call, message us on WhatsApp or fill the form on this website.

2. How the enquiry form works

The form on this website does not store your details on a server. When you press the button, it opens WhatsApp with a message containing your details, which you then send to our WhatsApp number. WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that message.

3. How we use information

  • To confirm your booking and arrange your vehicle and driver
  • To contact you about your trip, pickup or any change
  • To answer your questions and handle complaints
  • To meet legal, tax or accounting requirements

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share trip details such as your name, phone number and pickup point only with the driver assigned to your trip, and with authorities when the law requires it.

5. Third-party services

This website links to WhatsApp and loads fonts from Google Fonts. When you use them, those services’ own terms and privacy policies apply.

6. Cookies

This website does not set tracking cookies of its own. Embedded services such as Google Fonts may collect basic technical information when the page loads.

7. Data security

We take reasonable care to protect the information you share with us, but no method of sharing information is completely secure.

8. Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the contact details we hold about you by calling +91 95997 63796.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
